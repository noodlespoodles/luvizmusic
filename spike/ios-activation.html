    Give each tap about ten seconds before you judge it. The page waits seven seconds before saying BLOCKED, then keeps watching for another thirty, and will flip a BLOCKED back to PLAYING if the sound was only slow to arrive.

    Reading the result: PLAYING means the widget's playback position really moved forward, not merely that it claimed to start. BLOCKED means we asked it to play and the position never moved. If the sound is silent but the status says PLAYING, check the phone's mute switch and volume: that is a volume problem, not an activation problem.
